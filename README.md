# ALCBar AI Practice Risk Check

A short self-check for U.S. lawyers and law firms on how safely they use generative AI, from ALCBar.

## What it does

Claude asks 12 multiple-choice questions, about 4 minutes in total, then scores the answers out of 100 (higher is better). The result shows seven areas: ethics, confidentiality, verification, supervision, vendor risk, cybersecurity and competitive readiness. It names the three weakest areas with one practical next step each and links to the full report on ALCBar.com.

Claude can also give a short, general summary of the ethics duties covered by ABA Formal Opinion 512 (July 29, 2024): competence, confidentiality, communication, candor, supervision and fees.

## How to use it

Ask Claude something like:

- "Run the ALCBar AI practice risk check for my firm."
- "How safely is my law firm using AI?"
- "What does ABA Formal Opinion 512 say about confidentiality?"

## Data

The plugin connects to the ALCBar Risk Check server at https://mcp.alcbar.com/mcp. It sends only your 12 letter answers (a, b or c) or the ethics topic you ask about. The server scores the answers and returns the result. It stores nothing, needs no account or sign-in, and does not ask for client or matter information.

## Important

This is general education about practice management. It is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship. It does not decide whether you comply with any rule. ABA opinions are not binding; check your own jurisdiction's rules and guidance.
