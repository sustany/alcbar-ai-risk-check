# Privacy notice: ALCBar AI Practice Risk Check

Effective October 9, 2026

This notice covers the ALCBar AI Practice Risk Check plugin and its server at https://mcp.alcbar.com/mcp.

## What the server receives

- Your 12 answers to the risk check, each sent only as the letter a, b or c.
- Or the name of the ethics topic you ask about, such as "confidentiality".

The server does not ask for or receive your name, email address, firm name, client names or matter details.

## What happens to it

The server scores the answers, sends the result back to your AI assistant, and then discards them. It does not save answers or results, does not keep a log of requests, does not use cookies, and does not need an account or sign-in. Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is kept.

The server runs on a virtual private server hosted by Hostinger. Like any internet service, the hosting network may briefly process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver traffic and protect against attacks.

## Your AI assistant

Your conversation with Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant is handled by that company under its own privacy policy. ALCBar does not receive your conversation.

## The full report link

The result includes a link to https://alcbar.com/ai-practice-risk-score.html. If you open it, the ALCBar.com website privacy policy applies.
